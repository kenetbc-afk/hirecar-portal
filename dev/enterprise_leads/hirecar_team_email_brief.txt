Please narrow the A1/A2 leads first by service lane: vehicle/rental/fleet, delivery/logistics mobility, automotive referral partners, funding/credit-readiness, web/digital setup, and IT asset management. For each record, verify website/email and source confidence before deployment, then assign campaign owner, status, and next step.
